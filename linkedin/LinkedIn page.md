## About

> Foundation makes job hunting in the UK simpler.
>
> Instead of checking a dozen job boards every day, you save your searches once. Foundation checks Reed, Indeed, Totaljobs, CWJobs, CV-Library, Adzuna, LinkedIn, NHS Jobs, GOV.UK Find a Job, CharityJob and jobs.ac.uk for you, and tells you the moment a new job appears, so you can be one of the first to apply.
>
> • One search, every board: type a job and a town once, and Foundation sets it up on every board.
> • No repeats: the same job posted on several boards shows up once, and jobs you've deleted don't come back.
> • AI filters: say what you don't want in plain English, like "hide jobs under £30,000", and Foundation hides them.
> • CV builder: see what employers for your role ask for most, and build a CV around it.
>
> Free for Windows.

## Setting the page up (about 10 minutes)

1. On LinkedIn, click **For Business** (top right), then **Create a Company Page**, then **Company**.
2. Fill in the **Page details** above, and upload the logo.
3. Tick the box to confirm you can act for Foundation, then click **Create page**.
4. On the new page, click **Edit page**:
   - Add the cover image.
   - Paste the **About** text.
   - Add the specialties and the **Visit website** button.
5. Post the launch post.
