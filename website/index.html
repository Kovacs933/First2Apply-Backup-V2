Installing

  1. Open FoundationSetup.exe from your Downloads folder. Foundation installs and opens by itself.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because Foundation is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Click Sign up, confirm your email, and log in.

Mac and phone versions aren't available yet.

Questions

Is Foundation free?

Yes. Foundation is free to download and use, including the AI job filters.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No. Foundation finds new jobs and alerts you straight away. Click a job to open the advert and apply on the job board as usual.

Does my computer need to be on?

Yes. Foundation checks the job boards from your PC while it's running. It keeps going in the background when you close the window, and you can find it in the system tray next to the clock.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows shows a warning for new apps that aren't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info and then Run anyway to install.
